# Learning to Ask for Help

I used to think that asking for help meant I was behind everyone else. During my first semester, I kept rewriting the same introduction because I wanted it to be perfect. I didn't tell my instructor that I was stuck. Instead, I stayed up late and tried to solve the problem alone.

That changed when a classmate invited me to a writing group. We read one paragraph at a time and asked each other simple questions. Why did this example matter? What did the reader need to know first? Those questions helped me see the problem more clearly. I still wanted the paper to sound like me, but I could finally explain what I was trying to say.
